March Home Maintenance Checklist

5 tasks for March — each with the reason it's on the list. Print it, stick it on the fridge, and cross things off.

  • Every monthTest smoke and CO detectorsDetectors fail quietly — the only way to know one still works is to test it.
  • Once a yearClean the dryer vent ductLint in the exhaust duct is one of the leading causes of house fires, and a choked vent makes every load take two cycles instead of one..
  • Twice a year — before wet seasonsTest the sump pumpA sump pump spends most of its life idle and is only discovered dead during the storm that needed it..
  • Every 6 monthsInspect tub and shower caulkFailed caulk lets shower water into the wall cavity, where it rots framing and feeds mold invisibly.
  • Every 3 monthsDegrease the range hood filterA grease-saturated filter stops capturing cooking exhaust and becomes a fire risk sitting directly over the burners..

Press ⌘P (Mac) or Ctrl+P (Windows) to print this checklist.
