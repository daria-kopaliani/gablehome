How often should you shut off and drain outdoor faucets?

Every fall before first freeze. On a calendar: October.

Why it matters

Water trapped in an exterior faucet freezes, expands, and splits the pipe inside the wall — the leak only shows up the following spring.

How to do it

Close the interior shutoff valve for each hose bib, then open the outside tap and let it drain. Disconnect and drain garden hoses.

Signs you've waited too long

A hose bib that sprays inside the wall when first opened in spring means last winter already won.
