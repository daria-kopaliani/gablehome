How often should you trim branches away from the house?

Once a year. On a calendar: November.

Why it matters

Branches touching the roof abrade shingles with every breeze, drop debris into gutters, and give squirrels and ants a bridge into the house.

How to do it

Cut back anything touching or overhanging the roofline; late fall, with leaves down, is the easiest time to see structure. Leave big limbs near power lines to professionals.

Signs you've waited too long

Scratching sounds on the roof in wind, moss where a branch shades the shingles.
